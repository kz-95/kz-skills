# kz-uicheck - every check in full

What each check catches, with every finding code and skip attribute. `../SKILL.md` has the
short table, which check to run for what, and the `table-check.js` row in full.

| Check | Catches | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `checks/layout-audit.js` | Horizontal page overflow, overlapping boxes, clipped text, ragged control rows, tap targets under 44px, SVG text outside its viewBox or landing on other text, a focus ring on an SVG shape that is a bounding box instead of the shape (`FOCUS BOX`), a `var(--x)` that nothing defines so its whole declaration silently drops (`UNDEFINED TOKEN`), a sequence marked `data-orientation="horizontal"` that wraps and is taller than the area showing it (or wraps while marked `data-sequence="line"`), or overflows with no sideways scroll (`WRAPPED SEQUENCE`; opt a deliberately wrong sample out with `data-layout-check="skip"`), a fixed or sticky rail sitting on content, with the covered element and the pixel overlap (`COVERED`; dialogs, popovers, tooltips, toasts and full-width bars are exempt), a coloured stripe down one side of a box drawn as a wider border, a `::before` or `::after` bar or an inset shadow (`STRIPE`; the current nav item, a blockquote, table cells and form fields are exempt) | `V3`, `L5`, `L8`, `L10`, `L11`, `D6` |
| `checks/typing-check.js` | A field rebuilt mid-word (the phone keyboard closes), a caret thrown to the end, a field that moves while typed into, text under 16px that makes the browser zoom on focus | `F8` |
| `checks/popover-check.js` | A dropdown or panel that cannot be closed: its trigger only opens (`STUCK`), Escape or an outside click does nothing, no visible Done on touch | `N2` |
| `checks/contrast-check.js` | Text under 4.5:1 (3:1 large) against the background it really sits on, translucent layers and ancestor opacity composited, both themes; gradient backgrounds reported as `UNKNOWN`, never guessed | `Y2` |
| `checks/modal-check.js` | A modal that leaves the page scrolling (`SCROLL`), shifts it sideways (`SHIFT`), does not take focus, lets clicks through to the page behind (`BACKDROP`), cancels Escape, or does not return focus to its opener | `N1` |
| `checks/focus-check.js` | A control whose focus changes nothing visible (`NOFOCUS`), a focus indicator under 3:1 (`WEAK`), `tabindex` above 0, a clickable thing the keyboard cannot reach. Needs the page focused | `A` |
| `checks/motion-check.js` | `transition: all` (`ALL`), an ease-in curve (`EASEIN`), a transitioned width/height/top/margin (`LAYOUTPROP`), an entrance at `scale(0)` (`SCALE0`), a popover or menu growing from its own centre (`ORIGIN`), a button with no `:active` rule (`NOPRESS`); no reduced-motion query is an advisory. Reads styles only, safe on any page. Skip a deliberately wrong sample with `data-motion-check="skip"` | `M1` `M3`-`M5` |
| `checks/diagram-check.js` | An SVG diagram measured as drawn: too many boxes or lines (`BUDGET`), a slanted straight line (`DIAGONAL`), a line ending at a box corner (`CORNER`), two lines stacked (`STACKED`) or meeting a box at one point (`SHARED PORT`), a line behind a box that is not its end (`BEHIND BOX`), a label plate on its line (`LABEL ON LINE`), under 4px from one (`LABEL GAP`) or under a later box (`PLATE UNDER BOX`), a key inside the drawing (`KEY INSIDE`), a diagram shrunk below its native width (`SHRUNK`), making the page scroll (`PAGE SCROLL`) or cut off by a clipping parent (`CLIPPED`), scrolling with no visible cue (`NO CUE`), no accessible name (`NO NAME`, `DUP ID`), accent on more than two boxes (`EMPHASIS`). Run at a wide width and at 375px. Skip a deliberately wrong sample with `data-diagram-check="skip"` | `G1`-`G10` |
| `checks/form-check.js` | A field with no label, a phone or email field typed as text, an error shown while typing, a required field left with no nudge, a refused submit that does not land in the first empty field. Types into the page: reload after | `F1` `F2` |
| `checks/toast-check.js` | The toast system, measured by making toasts through the page's own function (`window.kzToast`, `toast` or `showToast`, called as `fn(text, { kind, undo })` and then as `fn({ text, kind, undo })`) or by pressing elements marked `data-kz-toast-test`; nothing else is ever pressed. A toast or toast region that is not a live region (`NO LIVE`), a toast with no dismiss (`NO CLOSE`), more than three on screen (`STACK`), the same message twice on screen twice (`RESTACK`), removed and mounted again (`REMOUNT`) or kept with no count (`NO COUNT`), a toast carrying Undo removed by the next toast (`UNDO GONE`), two undoable toasts merged (`UNDO MERGED`), an error leaving before an ordinary toast (`ERROR SHORT`), an ordinary toast gone in under 2s (`TOO FAST`), a toast underneath an open modal or sheet, measured with `elementFromPoint` after opening the first closed `<dialog>` with `showModal()` (`UNDER DIALOG`). No function and no marked trigger: static findings only, with a `NO HOOK` advisory. Dismisses every toast it made. Skip a deliberately wrong sample with `data-toast-check="skip"` | `A1` `A2` |
| `checks/explain-check.js` | Every icon-only button (an icon, an SVG or one glyph, and no visible words), asked to explain itself the way the device can. On a hovering pointer: nothing on hover or focus and no `[?]` within 48px (`NO EXPLAIN`; a `title` alone counts as nothing), hover but not focus (`HOVER ONLY`), focus but not hover (`FOCUS ONLY`), and with one tooltip open the neighbour's taking over 100ms (`SLOW NEXT`). On a device that cannot hover: no long-press explanation and no `[?]` (`NO TOUCH PATH`). Either: no accessible name (`NO NAME`); a tooltip that only repeats the name is an advisory (`REPEATS NAME`). CSS `:hover` reveals are read from the stylesheet, since a script cannot hover. Headless Chrome reports no pointer, which reads as touch: emulate a pointer for the hover path. Skips dismiss glyphs named Close or Dismiss and the `[?]` toggles themselves; skip a deliberately wrong sample with `data-explain-check="skip"` | `B1` `B2` `M6` |
| `checks/agree-check.js` | One quantity shown in two places, or a total and its parts, read from what the page shows. The page marks every element that shows a quantity `data-quantity="<name>"`, a total `data-total="<name>"` and its parts `data-part-of="<name>"`; the check reads the first number in each (thousands separators, a currency sign, a leading minus, U+2212 or a bracketed negative), hidden screens included. `DISAGREE` when one quantity shows two values, `SUM` when the parts do not add up at the most decimals any of them shows (33.33 x 3 under 100.00 fails), `NO NUMBER`, `NO PARTS`; `NO MARKS` advisory when nothing is marked. Opt a wrong sample out with `data-agree-check="skip"` | `V4` |
| `checks/colour-check.js` | What a colour is allowed to say. Resolves the page's own tokens through the cascade: the accent (`--accent`, else `--primary`, `--brand`) and the status tokens (`--ok`, `--success`, `--good`, `--up`, `--warn`, `--warning`, `--danger`, `--error`, `--bad`, `--down` and synonyms). `ACCENT STATIC`: text in the accent, or on an accent background, on an element that cannot be acted on and is not inside one. `NEAR STATUS`: an identity colour (`data-identity="<name>"`, one name counted once) within 30 degrees of hue of a saturated status colour; advisory when the element carries `data-user-colour`. `TOO CLOSE`: two identities under delta-E 18 (CIE76). SVG and canvas are left to C7. Opt a wrong sample out with `data-colour-check="skip"` | `Y4` |
